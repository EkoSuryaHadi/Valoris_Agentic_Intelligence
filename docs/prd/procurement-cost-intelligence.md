# Module 06 — Procurement Cost Intelligence PRD

## Vision

Deliver trusted intelligence for PO, vendor exposure, material pricing, commitments, delivery risk, and escalation.

## Problem

Project teams need consistent, traceable, decision-ready information instead of fragmented spreadsheets and disconnected source records.

## Primary users

Project controls, project managers, commercial and finance teams, executives, reviewers, and platform administrators.

## Goals

1. Establish a governed source of truth for PO, vendor exposure, material pricing, commitments, delivery risk, and escalation.
2. Connect results to project, WBS/CBS, period, currency, and provenance context.
3. Make assumptions, calculation basis, confidence, and ownership visible.
4. Support human review for material decisions.

## Scope

PO, vendor exposure, material pricing, commitments, delivery risk, and escalation; module workflows; approvals and audit; imports and integrations; drill-down evidence; alerts and reporting contracts.

## Out of scope

Unapproved autonomous financial posting, bypassing authorization, and functionality owned by other modules.

## Requirements

- Versioned inputs and outputs.
- Source and freshness metadata on material records.
- Explainable calculations and thresholds.
- Role- and project-scoped access.
- Immutable audit history for material decisions.
- Reproducible period snapshots.

## Success criteria

Users can trace each material result to its inputs, assumptions, source, owner, and review state. Exceptions are surfaced early and routed to the correct reviewer.
