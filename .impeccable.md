## Design Context

### Users
Cost Engineers, Project Controls, Project Managers, Finance/Commercial users, and executives working in project cost-control workspaces. Their job is to establish trusted project structure and baseline data, spot cost movement early, and route decisions to the authorized human reviewer.

### Brand Personality
Operational, assured, evidence-first. The interface should feel calm under pressure, precise in dense data, and clear about what is advisory versus approved.

### Aesthetic Direction
Industrial editorial workspace: warm paper surfaces, ink navy structure, amber signal accents, restrained red for risk, high-density tables, and visible provenance metadata. Light-first with future dark-mode support. Avoid chatbot-first layouts, neon AI gradients, and decorative dashboard cards.

### Design Principles
1. Context is always visible: organization, project, period, role, and freshness.
2. Evidence before interpretation: show source and status beside every material value.
3. Calm hierarchy: use color to signal action, not to decorate every component.
4. Safe progression: preview, validate, review, then commit or approve.
5. Human control is explicit: agents recommend; authorized humans decide.
